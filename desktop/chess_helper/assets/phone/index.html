Connecting

Connect to your computer

On your computer, open BoardLens → Phone and enable the companion. Enter the six-digit code shown there.

Your computer and phone need to use the same local network. The computer does the screen reading and engine work.

Waiting for a board

Open a complete chessboard on your computer, then press Scan now.
