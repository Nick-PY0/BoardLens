BOARDLENS 1.5 — DESKTOP 2.12

One Android app, two separate sections:

ON THIS PHONE
Scan a complete 2D board in another Android app. Android 10+, arm64.
Open Phone engines and download the optional 99 MB Stockfish model.
Press Start and approve full-screen capture. Allow display over other apps
for touch-through arrows. Stop from the app or the ongoing notification.
Phone settings save automatically and never overwrite the desktop settings.

Choose On phone for local Stockfish. Choose Computer in Phone engines for
installed desktop Maia, Flounder, Tiny bots or custom UCI engines analyzing
your phone's recognized position. Pair Computer companion first. Only the
recognized position is sent to that computer; screenshots are never sent.
Computer-backed engines are not native Android ports.

COMPUTER COMPANION
Desktop → Phone → Enable phone companion. On the same Wi-Fi, enter the
computer address and six-digit pairing code. This section controls desktop
boards/settings; edits sync both ways and save automatically. A phone or
tablet browser can also open the same address. Keep the computer running.

EVALUATION AND PREDICTIONS
Choose a separate evaluation engine in desktop/companion Engine settings.
Stockfish may score the bar while Maia recommends moves. Early suggestions
work as soon as each provider returns its first result; one-move providers
then predict the opponent's reply and your following move. These are
predictions, not guaranteed play. Each extra position uses its search budget.
Local phone evaluation uses its own maximum-strength Stockfish session.

UPDATES
Desktop automatically checks/downloads verified updates and applies them on
next launch. Turn this off in Board → App. Android can automatically check
for releases and open GitHub; Android controls installation. No custom APK
installer or install-packages permission is included. Existing Android 9+
installations can migrate to the release signing certificate through Android
key rotation without uninstalling. Keep the app's data when updating.
