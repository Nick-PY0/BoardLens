BOARDLENS ANDROID 1.7.0 / CODE 9

PHONE SCREEN — NO COMPUTER NEEDED
Open BoardLens → Phone screen → Settings → Models & downloads.
Twelve Tiny bots are built in. Download Stockfish's 99 MB model for the default
move engine/evaluation, or choose optional Maia 2 / Maia 3 5M, 23M or 79M.
Select recommendation and evaluation engines separately. Settings save.
Models → Test installed engines checks real engine execution on your device.

Press Start, approve entire-screen sharing, and enable Display over other apps
for arrows. Press Home or Open another app. Keep all 64 squares visible.
A foreground-service session continues when the interface is in the background
or dismissed. Stop in the app or notification ends it. Android requires new
sharing approval for each session. Locking, force-stop, revoked access and OS
restrictions can interrupt capture. Protected screens cannot be read.

COMPUTER COMPANION — OPTIONAL, SEPARATE SECTION
Desktop → Phone → Enable companion. Use the same trusted Wi-Fi and enter the
computer address and six-digit code. Desktop settings sync with this dashboard.
Phone-screen settings are separate and never change the desktop engine/game.

ENGINE CONTROLS
Stockfish: time, depth, threads, skill and ranked alternatives.
Maia: one-pass human-move prediction, target rating and CPU threads.
Tiny bots: one move, coded depth, adjustable clock budget.
Predict replies: possible future moves. Three plies = your move, opponent reply,
your response. Reply budget limits additional passes, not an artificial delay.
Steady arrows reduces switching; Lock move holds one until a real change.
Only my color follows the side at the bottom of the recognized board.

LIMITS
Android 10+ / ARM64 for local analysis. Recognition is experimental for unusual
artwork and projected 3D boards; all themes/angles are not guaranteed. A fresh
midgame scan may need the side-to-move override. No Accessibility service,
screen recording/upload, autoplay, Study or coaching.

Automatic checks find new Android releases. Android asks before installation.
This signed APK passes host/package checks, but A35 installation, live capture
and background behavior for this exact release still need physical testing.
Keep normal Android security checks enabled.
